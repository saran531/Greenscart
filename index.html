GREENSCART

From Agricultural Value to Everyday Nutrition

Innovating Food. Creating Value. Enabling Better Choices.

Greenscart is a food and agri-value-addition brand from Madurai, Tamil Nadu, focused on manufacturing, processing, repacking and developing food products across cereals, flours, oils, spices, snacks, beverages and other food categories.

The business was established as GreensCart in 2019 and has since evolved into GRCTS Food Manufacturer Private Limited, with food manufacturing as its major business activity. The company is registered as a Micro enterprise under Udyam.

Registered FSSAI Food Category Scope

Our FSSAI licence covers a broad range of food activities, including manufacturing, repacking and retail, with registered products/categories covering oils, biscuits, macaroni, atta, besan, semolina, spices, cereal products, snacks, beverages, nuts and several other food categories.

LEADERSHIP & VISION

Sankaralingam K

Founder & CEO, GRCTS Food Manufacturer Private Limited

Sankaralingam K is an entrepreneur with expertise in business development, strategic planning, leadership, market analysis, collaboration and partnerships. He holds a Bachelor of Engineering and an MBA.

His entrepreneurial journey includes recognition through STARTUP MANIA 4.0, TNSI, JALLIKATTU 2.0, Startup Weekend, the Dr. Kalam Young Achiever Award and the Global Student Entrepreneur Awards.

He has also served as a speaker and trainer in entrepreneurship and value-added products, participated in multiple incubation programmes, acted as a jury member for 15+ State and Central Government events, and served as a guest speaker at 300+ events.
